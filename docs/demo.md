# Kolehiyo Central demo

A laptop walkthrough for Eng for Professionals. The demo opens already signed in as one sample applicant. Code is not what the class reviews.

## Applicant

Jordan Santos, Grade 12, Philippine High School, grade average 94.

## Screens

1. **Directory** of the Big Four, in this order: UP, Ateneo, DLSU, UST. Each row shows the entrance test, application period, exam dates, and release of results.
2. **University page**: a short introduction, four programs (name and one sentence), the fee note, and the checklist. The UP page also states that the real UPCAT asks for two campuses and up to four programs per campus. Jordan’s UP choice stays one program, BS Computer Science at UP Diliman. The program list is for reading. The chosen program is the seeded one.
3. **Applications**: university, chosen program, and status. One note on the page: ask Philippine High School for the school record as soon as applications open, and tell Ateneo’s two teachers to expect the portal email.
4. **Profile**: name, Philippine High School, and grade average.

## Calendar

## Programs

- **UP Diliman:** BS Computer Science, BS Biology, BS Business Administration, BA Journalism
- **Ateneo:** BS Management Engineering, BS Computer Science, BS Biology, BS Management Information Systems
- **DLSU Manila:** BS Computer Science (Software Technology), BS Accountancy, BS Biology, AB Communication Arts
- **UST:** BS Pharmacy, BS Medical Technology, BS Accountancy, BA Communication

## Checklist

Lines the applicant can tick. Marking them done is the interaction. Status follows the ticks: none is Not started, some is In progress, all is Submitted. Submitted means the list is finished inside Kolehiyo Central. The university has not received the application.

Every university:

- PSA birth certificate
- ID photo (the UST line notes the nameplate tag)
- Grade 12 school ID
- LRN
- That university’s school-record form: UP Form 2 for Grades 8–11, Ateneo’s record for Grades 9–11, DLSU’s scholastic record and Form 138, or the USTET grades form for Grades 9–11

Also:

- UP Form 1
- Ateneo: “Personal essay drafted” and “Two recommenders named”
- DLSU: parent consent, because a Grade 12 student is under 18. DLSU has no essay and no recommendations.

## Fee note

Shown on each university page. It is not a checklist line, and the applicant does not pay in the demo.

- **UP:** Filipino applicants pay no UPCAT fee.
- **Ateneo, DLSU, and UST:** about ₱600–₱1,000, sometimes waived.

## How the story starts

- **UP:** the four shared lines done, Form 1 and Form 2 open. In progress. BS Computer Science.
- **Ateneo:** the four shared lines done; school record, essay, and recommenders open. In progress. BS Management Engineering.
- **DLSU:** every line done. Submitted. BS Accountancy.
- **UST:** nothing done. Not started. No program yet.

## If time is left

One admin screen that edits a university’s application period. The directory shows the new months. Jordan’s checklist stays as it was. There is no staff login per university and no review of the checklist.

## Outside this demo

Sign-up, file upload, an essay box, fee payment, scholarships, transfers, reminders that send themselves, a confirmation slip, a phone layout, and a staff review of Jordan’s file.

# Kolehiyo Central

The class demo of a single place where a Filipino grade 12 student looks up freshman admission to the Big Four and tracks those applications.

## Language

**Kolehiyo Central**:
The platform the demo walks through.
_Avoid_: Common App, UCAS

**University**:
One institution an applicant can apply to. In this demo, each university is a single school entry.
_Avoid_: College, school, HEI

**Big Four**:
The only universities in the demo: the University of the Philippines, Ateneo de Manila, De La Salle University Manila, and the University of Santo Tomas.
_Avoid_: UAAP schools, NCAA schools

**Applicant**:
A grade 12 student preparing freshman college applications.
_Avoid_: User, student, account

**Program**:
A freshman degree offering shown under a university. Programs shown for the University of the Philippines are UP Diliman programs.
_Avoid_: Course, major

**Entrance test**:
The freshman admission exam a university requires: UPCAT for UP, ACET for Ateneo, DCAT for DLSU, and USTET for UST.
_Avoid_: Exam, assessment

**Application period**:
The months when a university accepts freshman applications.
_Avoid_: Deadline, due date

**Exam dates**:
The months when a university holds its entrance test.
_Avoid_: Test day, schedule

**Release of results**:
The months when a university releases freshman admission decisions.
_Avoid_: Application status, admission result

**Application fee**:
The amount a university charges to apply, shown as a note. UP’s note is that Filipino applicants pay no UPCAT fee.
_Avoid_: Payment, checkout

**Application**:
An applicant's freshman application to one university.
_Avoid_: Submission, form

**Profile**:
The applicant's name, senior high school, and grade average, shared by all of their applications.
_Avoid_: Account, biodata

**Requirement**:
One item on a university's freshman checklist. The applicant marks it done, including documents they still have to obtain from their senior high school.
_Avoid_: Upload, file, attachment

**Application status**:
How far an application has gotten on its checklist: Not started when none are done, In progress when some are done, and Submitted when all are done.
_Avoid_: Pending, accepted, rejected

**Submitted**:
The application status in which every requirement for that university is marked done in Kolehiyo Central. The university has not received the application.
_Avoid_: Filed, sent, received
